BEST PRACTICE PROTOCOLS

10 Simple Cybersecurity Rules Every Student Should Follow

Incorporate these ten foundational defensive principles into your daily academic and digital routines to stay shielded against cyber hazards.

01

Strong & Unique Passwords

Use long passphrases with 14+ characters. Never reuse passwords across academic and personal services.

02

Enable Two-Factor Auth

Turn on 2FA using authenticator apps on your college email, cloud storage, GitHub, and social portals.

03

Never Share Passwords

Do not share credentials with roommates or classmates. Use delegation or cloud folder sharing instead.

04

Check Links Before Clicking

Hover over links to verify the actual destination URL before clicking any urgent link in emails or chats.

05

Beware Unknown Attachments

Never open unsolicited .exe, .zip, or macro-enabled documents claiming to be class schedules or scholarships.

06

Keep Software Updated

Regularly patch your operating system, web browsers, and antivirus software to close dangerous zero-day holes.

07

Guard on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid online banking or sensitive logins over public networks. Use a trusted encrypted VPN service.

08

Download from Official Sources

Avoid cracked software and torrents. Download applications strictly from verified vendors and app stores.

09

Protect Social Privacy

Keep profiles private. Don't post student IDs, admit cards, birthdates, or real-time location check-ins.

10

Report Suspicious Activity

Notify campus IT or official cyber safety cells immediately if you witness phishing attacks or data breaches.

ACADEMIC PROJECT OVERVIEW

About CyberShield Initiative

CyberShield is an academic research and education platform dedicated to evaluating cybersecurity awareness, password habits, and digital hygiene practices among modern university students.

With university environments increasingly targeted by automated credential stuffing, phishing campaigns, and malware vectors, this platform provides actionable self-assessment alongside structured educational micro-modules.

Empirical Analysis

Captures nuanced user behavioral trends across multi-department cohorts.

Tailored Feedback

Scores responses immediately and provides targeted recommendations.

Project Deliverables & Compliance

  • Zero External Frameworks: Built purely with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JavaScript, and Chart.js.
  • LocalStorage Architecture: Stores multiple survey submissions persistently without any backend dependencies.
  • Comprehensive Survey: 16 carefully formulated questions evaluating credentials, 2FA, phishing, and software patching.
  • Multi-dimensional Charts: 6 dynamic charts updating instantly upon new survey entry.
  • Interactive Knowledge Suite: Real-time searchable modules and a 5-question defensive quiz.
